The four program buckets above are only manageable if something holds them together. APAS provides day-to-day project management for the crews on site and program-level management across all four buckets — scope control, change management, schedule, pay application review, permit tracking and regulatory correspondence. All of it is instantiated in ProjOS, so that every project carries its scope, deliverables, permits, documents and current status in one operating record the owner can open at any time.

Why this matters

Thirty-one projects across four systems and three regulators is not a spreadsheet problem. Without a single operating record, the owner is reconstructing the truth from email every time a question is asked.

Program & Project Management Services

Day-to-day management of the crews on site and program-level control across all four buckets.

Thirty-one projects, four systems, three regulators and multiple contractors do not coordinate themselves. This is the function that holds scope and delivery under control across the whole program — and the function that keeps the owner from having to adjudicate between contractors.

Scope of Work
  • Day-to-day management of on-site crews and contractors: coordination, sequencing, quality and safety oversight.
  • Program-level scope control across all buckets, with interface management where projects touch.
  • Integrated master schedule with the cross-bucket dependency map maintained live.
  • Procurement and administration of all specialty subcontracts.
  • Change management, and change order review and negotiation.
  • Pay application review, verification against installed work, and lien waiver control.
  • Contract administration, including warranty tracking and retainage management.
  • Permit tracking and agency coordination across the City, DERM, RER, SFWMD and FDEP.
  • Owner reporting on a defined cadence: status, risk and schedule performance.
  • Risk register maintained with mitigation owners and dates.
Deliverables
  • Integrated master schedule, maintained live
  • Monthly owner report: status, risk and schedule performance
  • Change management log with full substantiation
  • Reviewed and certified pay applications
  • Risk register with mitigation owners
  • Permit and agency coordination record

ProjOS Program Operating Record

Every project in this program instantiated in ProjOS — one operating record the owner can open at any time.

This is the layer worth seeing rather than reading about, and it is the one we should walk through live with R4. Each project in this document becomes a live object in ProjOS carrying its scope, deliverables, permits, documents and current status. The systems connect: the flow meter feeds live data, the compliance calendar drives alerts, the evidence file assembles itself as work is completed. The owner stops asking what the status is and starts looking at it.

Scope of Work
  • Instantiate all program projects in ProjOS with scope, deliverables, dependencies and approval status.
  • Configure the five program buckets with roll-up reporting to a program-level dashboard.
  • Connect live data: the sewer flow meter, groundwater monitoring results and stormwater performance monitoring.
  • Build the compliance engine: permit register, reporting calendar, automated alerts and evidence capture.
  • Establish the document control system, with every drawing, permit, certification and report attached to its project.
  • Track every project proposal from issue through approval to completion, so the authorisation record is never in doubt.
  • Configure owner-facing dashboards for status, schedule and risk.
  • Build the operations layer for the stormwater, sewer and water operations plans, with scheduled tasks and completion evidence.
  • Demonstrate the system live to R4 and configure access.
Deliverables
  • All program projects live in ProjOS
  • Program dashboard with bucket roll-up
  • Live data integrations
  • Compliance engine with automated alerts and evidence capture
  • Document control system
  • Proposal and approval tracking
  • Owner-facing dashboards and access
  • Live demonstration to R4
