Invoice workflow should teach the user as it protects the company.

The flow below is the recommended implementation pattern for both consulting and construction. It keeps invoices from floating loose and makes the next step obvious to a non technical staff member.

Step 1

Project setup gate

No invoice can start until company, project type, client, proposal value, and billing method are loaded.

  • APAS Build or APAS Consulting
  • Construction pay app or consulting invoice
  • Owner and sender identity confirmed
Step 2

Proposal breakdown

Prime amount, consulting amount, subcontractor amount, allowances, and reimbursables are entered as the billing source.

  • Warn on missing scope
  • Track percent billed
  • Prevent over billing
Step 3

Create invoice

The user picks a percent, milestone, or line amount. Proj OS prorates subcontractor and consulting lines when allowed.

  • Drafts are editable
  • Errors can be deleted
  • Approved invoices lock
Step 4

Attach report

Reports, photos, evidence, and PDF attachments are collated into one client ready package with a side preview.

  • Preview before send
  • Email body generated
  • Download package
Step 5

Approval record

Admin override is required after approval. Every change keeps a visible audit note and approval trail.

  • Locked after approval
  • Admin change reason
  • Client delivery record
