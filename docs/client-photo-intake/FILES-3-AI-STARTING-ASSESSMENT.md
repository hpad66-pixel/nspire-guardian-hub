## Recommended human decisions before conversion into work items

1. Identify the exact building, courtyard, stall, gate, or asset for each group. GPS can place the pin, but a plain-language location is still needed.
2. Confirm which photographs are observations, which are “before,” and whether any are legitimate completion evidence.
3. Separate active work that is incomplete by design from damage or failed restoration.
4. Verify safety and accessibility priorities in the field; the photographs do not provide dimensions, depth, slope, energization, or code compliance.
5. Assign one accountable party and due date per confirmed condition, then require one to three “after” photographs from a comparable angle.
6. Keep AI text labeled as a suggestion until the uploader or project reviewer accepts it.
